My Page order status (Baemin-style tracker in Meat Mall style). Three stops: Захиалга → Төлбөр → Хүргэгдсэн. On load the bar fills and the rider drives to the current position (1.4s). Rider states: idle (pending), moving (processing), arrived (completed). Cancelled orders show no bar. Reload to replay.
